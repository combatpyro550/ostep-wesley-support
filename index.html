Ostep Is Not an Illegal Immigrant or Undocumented

Ostep came to the United States through a legal immigration process.

He has participated in the immigration process through the appropriate legal channels and has made every immigration court appearance required of him to date.

His current situation developed from complications surrounding his immigration status following the breakdown of his marriage. He is now facing an immigration court hearing on November 30, 2026 and needs qualified legal representation to navigate that process.

He is facing the immigration system—not avoiding it.

Ostep is seeking qualified legal representation to help him properly present his case and pursue a path forward through the legal system.

Ostep's Story

Ostep Wesley came to the United States approximately in 2009 as the companion of his wife, who came to the United States through a medical provider visa program.

During his years in the United States, Ostep worked hard to build a life here. He earned his Master's degree in Architecture from the University of Memphis and continued pursuing his education and professional career.

Ostep has also been a devoted member of the Calvary Chapel fellowship during his time in the United States. He has served with a joyful heart in many capacities, including fellowship and worship, greeting, cleaning, setup and takedown, events, and conferences.

Following the breakdown of his marriage, Ostep's immigration circumstances changed significantly. He lost his work authorization and has faced considerable difficulty moving forward professionally while trying to resolve his immigration situation.

The Circumstances Behind His Current Situation

Ostep's current immigration difficulties developed in the aftermath of the breakdown of his marriage.

During that period, Ostep experienced an altercation with his former wife in Fargo, North Dakota, during which he was physically injured. Ostep chose not to pursue criminal charges at the time.

Following the separation and divorce proceedings, significant changes occurred in Ostep's immigration circumstances. His understanding is that his name was removed from the Green Card application without his knowledge after separation from his wife, and that untrue statements and accusations made about him during and after the divorce contributed to difficulty obtaining employment and educational opportunities.

Because he chose not to pursue criminal charges following the altercation, there is limited independent documentation concerning that incident, and the evidence that does exist of his physical assault is locked behind red-tape under Marsy's Law. These circumstances are part of the background that his attorney will need to evaluate as she prepares his immigration case.

Our purpose in sharing this information is not to make legal findings concerning anyone involved. We are explaining the circumstances as we understand them so that people can understand why Ostep needs legal representation now.

Loss of Work Authorization

Ostep's immigration circumstances have significantly affected his ability to work in the United States. Following the breakdown of his marriage and changes to his immigration application, Ostep lost his work authorization.

Despite earning a Master's degree in Architecture from the University of Memphis and working hard to establish himself professionally, the loss of his work authorization has made it legally impossible for him to maintain stable employment and support himself.

His Education and Career

Ostep earned his Master's degree in Architecture from the University of Memphis and has continued working toward a career in architecture despite the challenges surrounding his immigration situation.

He has been offered architecture internships and has completed an architecture internship. However, the complexity and uncertainty surrounding his immigration situation have made it difficult to find an architecture firm willing and able to support the immigration process necessary for him to advance his career.

Despite these obstacles, Ostep has continued pursuing his professional goals.

Ostep's goal is to use the education, experience, and skills he has developed during his years in the United States to build a career in architecture and give back to the country that has been his home for so long.
